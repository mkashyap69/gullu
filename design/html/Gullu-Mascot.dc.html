MEET YOUR MONEY BUDDY

Gullu

Holds your coins. Keeps your plan honest.

Named after the gullak, the clay piggy bank almost every Indian kid filled one coin at a time. Gullu sits at the centre of the app: he catches your payday money, sends it down the waterfall, and tells you in one line where you stand.

On track
“We’re 2 months early. Nice and steady.”
Payday
“₹55,000 just landed. Shall we drop it in?”
What if
“Just trying ideas. Nothing changes until you say so.”
Needs attention
“We’re ₹3.44 Cr short. Want 3 ways to fix it?”
Waiting
“No payday yet. I’ll nudge you tomorrow.”
Goal reached
“We did it! ₹1.50 Cr. Every month counted.”
HOW GULLU TALKS
  • Says “we”. One line at a time, plain words.
  • Cheers your habits: placing money on time, a full safety net, a maxed PPF year.
  • Stays calm when markets dip: “The plan expected some of this.”
  • Never names funds or stocks, never promises returns, never celebrates market luck.
  • Never uses timers, prizes, guilt or comparisons with other people.
